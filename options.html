Deck Constructor

While enabled, clicking the “Save Deck” button will remember which Card Pool was active. When you load this deck up in the future, that Card Pool will be automatically selected.

Card rule sets

Choose formats. Expand a set to browse its cards.

Import list

Validation optionally downloads Duelingbook’s card catalog for this check. The catalog is not bundled or saved.

Local development

Watch local JSON while this tab stays open. Valid saves update live; your formats and enabled state stay intact.

Card selection

Show a confirmation panel before applying card rule selections. Off by default: click a card to use it immediately. Multi-card effects still let you finish with fewer cards than the maximum.

Hand shuffle

Automatically shuffle your hand after your first draw in your own Draw Phase, once per turn.

Wait briefly after your last draw and until your pointer moves away from the deck and draw menu. Further draws restart the wait. Manually shuffling, changing your hand, leaving the tab, or waiting over 20 seconds cancels it. When both options are enabled, this delayed behavior takes priority.

Spell and trap costs

Pay clear fixed or half-LP costs when you activate your own Spell or Trap. Supports modern text and simple older wording used in Edison. Skips ambiguous effects, effect choices, upkeep, monster effects, and Apply effect actions. Other costs remain manual. Payment is immediate; do not also subtract it manually. Use the normal LP controls to correct a payment.

Battle damage

Show a confirmation for battle damage you owe, including Honest and Kalut adjustments. LP changes only after you confirm.

Turn order

Choose First whenever Dueling Book lets you select the turn order, including after siding.

Rock Paper Scissors

Show the automation modes during RPS. Turn this off to hide the controls and disable all automatic picks.

Modes are also available on the RPS screen. Clicking Automatic there makes a random pick immediately and saves the mode for future duels. Manual and Random on tie wait for your first card choice.
