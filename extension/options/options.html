Rooms to display

Host a Duel Defaults

Choose the starting values for Host a Duel. Enable Reuse last hosted duel setup to update these defaults after you host.

Options

When on, your most recently hosted duel setup becomes the default for the next time you host. When off, hosting does not update these defaults.

Deck Constructor

Control whether loading a deck also restores its card search filter.

Card Pool is the filter in Deck Constructor that limits which cards appear in searches, such as Edison or Goat. When on, saving a deck remembers its selected filter and loading that deck restores it. When off, saving does not remember a filter and loading does not restore one. Existing saved filters are kept. Your deck contents are unchanged.

Automation

Choose what the enhancer handles when opening Dueling Book and during a duel.

Intro

Enabled by default. Skip Dueling Book's opening animation and go straight to the login or front page. Turn off to watch the intro.

Card rule sets

Import list

Validation optionally downloads Duelingbook’s card catalog for this check. The catalog is not bundled or saved.

Local development

Watch local JSON while this tab stays open. Valid saves update live; your formats and enabled state stay intact.

Card selection

Show a confirmation panel before applying card rule selections. Off by default: click a card to use it immediately. Multi-card effects still let you finish with fewer cards than the maximum.

Hand shuffle

When on, shuffle your hand after the first draw in your own Draw Phase, once per turn. When off, this turn-draw shuffle is disabled. The separate setting below can still shuffle after draws.

When on, wait until you stop drawing and move your pointer away from the deck and draw menu, then shuffle your hand. Works on either player's turn. More draws restart the wait. Manually shuffling, changing your hand, leaving the tab, or waiting over 20 seconds cancels the pending shuffle. This takes priority over turn-draw shuffling. When off, only the separate turn-draw setting above can shuffle automatically.

Spell and trap costs

When on, immediately subtract a clear fixed or half-LP activation cost for your own Spell or Trap, without a confirmation. Do not subtract that cost again manually. Ambiguous costs, effect choices, upkeep, monster effects, and Apply effect actions remain manual. Use the normal LP controls to correct a payment. When off, pay all LP costs yourself.

Battle damage

When on, calculate battle damage you owe and show a prompt, including Honest and Kalut adjustments. Your LP changes only if you confirm. When off, this feature shows no prompts and makes no LP adjustments; calculate and apply battle damage yourself.

Turn order

When on, choose First whenever Dueling Book gives you the turn-order choice, including after siding. Enabling this while the choice is visible chooses First immediately. When off, choose First or Second yourself.

Rock Paper Scissors

When on, show the mode controls on the Rock Paper Scissors screen and use the mode selected below. When off, hide those controls and disable all automatic picks; choose each round yourself.

Modes are also available on the RPS screen. Clicking Automatic there makes a random pick immediately and saves the mode for future duels. Manual and Random on tie wait for your first card choice.

Card Pools

Choose the Card Pools available in Deck Constructor. Drag or use the arrows to reorder; use the eye to show or hide a pool.

Custom Sort

Set the priority of Custom Sort rules. Higher rules take priority; uncheck a rule to skip it.

Custom Colors

Customize Dueling Book's colors and backgrounds. Each Reset button restores that color's default.

Grid lines: on shows the grid over Dueling Book's background; off hides it.
